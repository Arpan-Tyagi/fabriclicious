# Fabriclicious: The Atelier Editorial Copy

## PILLAR 1: SEARCH OPTIMIZATION PROTOCOLS (SEO, AEO & GEO)
### 1. Traditional SEO (Search Engine Optimization)
**Metadata Formulas:**
- **Title Tag:** [Textile Name / Page Title] | [Weave / Key Attribute] | Fabriclicious Atelier (Strictly under 60 characters).
- **Meta Description:** [Primary USP] + [GSM & Composition] + [Cut policy / Swatch promise] + [CTA] (Strictly 150–160 characters).
- **Semantic Hierarchy:** One `<h1>` per view, logical `<h2>` topic clusters, and `<h3>` technical sub-parameters.
- **Contextual Internal Linking:** Anchor links between catalog listings, textile educational glossaries, and the Swatch Studio using exact descriptive anchor text.

### 2. AEO (Answer Engine Optimization — Snippets, Voice & PAA)
**Direct-Answer Passage Architecture:** 
Every major section and product category must feature clear conversational questions as headings (e.g., "How much fabric is required for a bias-cut slip dress?").

**40–60 Word Fact Blocks:** 
Immediately beneath each question heading, provide an objective, authoritative direct answer in 40 to 60 words before expanding into detailed narrative. This format directly feeds Google's Featured Snippets, Siri/Google Assistant voice queries, and PAA accordions.

### 3. GEO (Generative Engine Optimization — ChatGPT, Perplexity & AI Overviews)
**High Information-Gain Density:** 
Large Language Models prioritize unique, verifiable data points over repetitive marketing prose. Use quantitative attributes throughout: exact GSM numbers, usable bolt widths in centimeters, thread twist, momme weight, and shrinkage tolerances.

**Definitive Entity Definitions:** 
Format core concepts as authoritative definitions (e.g., "Normandy linen is a bast fiber textile retted naturally in river water...") so generative models can summarize the text without factual loss.

**Machine-Readable Attribute Tables:** 
Use standardized tabular comparisons (GSM vs. Silhouette, Needle Size vs. Fiber Weight) to simplify extraction for AI scrapers.

**Author Attribution & Provenance:** 
Cite European mill regions (Biella, Como, Lyon, Flanders) and master-tailor practices to build E-E-A-T signals (Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness).
